1 Platform Usage & Sovereignty

Access to the National Services Portal ("The Platform") is granted solely for the execution of official civil, legal, and administrative procedures within the jurisdiction of the République Tunisienne. Users must utilize the Platform in accordance with national laws governing digital identity and electronic communication.

Any unauthorized access, attempt to manipulate data, or circumvention of security protocols constitutes a violation of national cybersecurity mandates and is subject to immediate legal action under Sovereign Authority regulations.

2 Data Privacy & Transparency

We operate under a mandate of Modern Transparency. Personal data collected during registration and subsequent use of the Platform is processed strictly for the execution of requested administrative services.

  • Data is encrypted at rest and in transit using advanced national cryptographic standards.
  • Information is shared between authorized governmental entities only when strictly necessary for service fulfillment.
  • Citizens retain the right to audit their access logs via the 'Security Log' feature available in the user dashboard.

3 Digital Signature & Liability

Actions performed on this Platform, including the submission of forms and acceptance of terms, carry the equivalent legal weight of a physical signature under the Digital Economy Act.

Note on Liability: The user is solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their Secure Session credentials. The State is not liable for unauthorized actions resulting from user negligence.

4 Accessibility & Inclusion

The République Tunisienne is committed to ensuring equal access to digital services. This platform integrates adaptive technologies, including the omnipresent Sign Language Module (TSL), to facilitate communication for all citizens.
